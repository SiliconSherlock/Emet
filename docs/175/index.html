C-175

Forgiveness of sins comes only through believing in the name of Jesus.

  • Acts 10:43
  • Acts 4:12
  • John 3:18

More claims from: Isaiah · Ezekiel · Jonah · Psalms · Proverbs · Chronicles II · John · Acts

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

Acts 10:43

KJV
43To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins.

KJV
12Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.

John 3:18

KJV
18He that believeth on him is not condemned: but he that believeth not is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God.
