C-094

The promise that all nations would be blessed in Abraham's seed is the gospel preached beforehand.

  • Galatians 3:8
  • Acts 3:25
  • Genesis 12:3
  • Genesis 22:18

More claims from: Genesis · Acts · Galatians

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Galatians 3:8

KJV
8And the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham, saying, In thee shall all nations be blessed.

Acts 3:25

KJV
25Ye are the children of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying unto Abraham, And in thy seed shall all the kindreds of the earth be blessed.

Genesis 12:3

KJV
3And I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that curseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.

Genesis 22:18

KJV
18And in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed; because thou hast obeyed my voice.
