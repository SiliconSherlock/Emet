C-021

No one is righteous; all have sinned and cannot be righteous before God.

  • Romans 3:10-12
  • Romans 3:23
  • Psalms 14:1-3

More claims from: Genesis · Deuteronomy · Psalms · Job · Romans

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Romans 3:10-12

KJV
10As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one: 11There is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh after God. 12They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable; there is none that doeth good, no, not one.

Romans 3:23

KJV
23For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;

Psalms 14:1-3

KJV
1The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God. They are corrupt, they have done abominable works, there is none that doeth good. 2The LORD looked down from heaven upon the children of men, to see if there were any that did understand, and seek God. 3They are all gone aside, they are all together become filthy: there is none that doeth good, no, not one.
