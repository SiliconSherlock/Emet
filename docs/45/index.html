C-045

Psalm 118:22, the stone which the builders refused, speaks of Jesus.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Matthew 21:42 KJV

42Jesus saith unto them, Did ye never read in the scriptures, The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner: this is the Lord’s doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes?

Acts 4:11 KJV

11This is the stone which was set at nought of you builders, which is become the head of the corner.

Psalms 118:22-23 KJV

22The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner. 23This is the LORD’S doing; it is marvellous in our eyes.
