C-030

Habakkuk 2:4 teaches that the just shall live by faith, not by works of the Law.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Romans 1:17 KJV

17For therein is the righteousness of God revealed from faith to faith: as it is written, The just shall live by faith.

Galatians 3:11 KJV

11But that no man is justified by the law in the sight of God, it is evident: for, The just shall live by faith.

Habakkuk 2:4 KJV

4Behold, his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him: but the just shall live by his faith.
