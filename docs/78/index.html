C-078

Isaiah 53:8, 'he was cut off out of the land of the living', foretells the death of the Messiah.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Acts 8:33 KJV

33In his humiliation his judgment was taken away: and who shall declare his generation? for his life is taken from the earth.

Isaiah 53:8 KJV

8He was taken from prison and from judgment: and who shall declare his generation? for he was cut off out of the land of the living: for the transgression of my people was he stricken.
