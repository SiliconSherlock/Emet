C-186

God has given a further revelation through his Son that completes the Law and the Prophets.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

John 1:17 KJV

17For the law was given by Moses, but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.

Matthew 5:17 KJV
