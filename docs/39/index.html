C-039

The Scriptures foretold that the Messiah would rise on the third day.

  • 1 Corinthians 15:3-4
  • Matthew 12:40
  • Hosea 6:2
  • Jonah 1:17

More claims from: Hosea · Jonah · Matthew · 1 Corinthians
