Finding claims

  • By number. Type 42 and press Enter. In a debate, the address /42 works too.
  • By words or verse. virgin, suffering servant, Isaiah 53. Every word must appear; matches are highlighted.
  • By book. Choose a book, then whether the claim cites it or is answered from it.
  • Keys. / to search, ↑ ↓ to step through the results.

Reading a claim

  • The verse the claim cites, in Hebrew and JPS 1917 English.
  • The verses around it, folded; the cited verse is highlighted.
  • Other TaNaKh passages on the same question.
