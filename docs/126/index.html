C-126

Psalm 132:11, 'Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne', is God's oath to raise up Christ.

  • Acts 2:30
  • Psalms 132:11

More claims from: Psalms · Acts

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Acts 2:30

KJV
30Therefore being a prophet, and knowing that God had sworn with an oath to him, that of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne;

Psalms 132:11

KJV
11The LORD hath sworn in truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne.
