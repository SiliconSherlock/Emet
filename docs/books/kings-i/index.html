Claims answered from Kings I

Passages from Kings I are listed in answer to these claims.

  1. C-020Without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness of sin.Kings I 8:46-50
  2. C-092God's promise to David in 2 Samuel 7:12-16, 'I will be his father, and he shall be my son', is spoken of Jesus.Kings I 8:20
  3. C-144Genesis 19:24, 'the LORD rained brimstone from the LORD out of heaven', shows two persons called the LORD.Kings I 8:1
  4. C-146The Hebrew word for God, Elohim, is plural and so points to more than one person in God.Kings I 11:33
  5. C-147God says 'us' in Genesis 3:22 and 11:7 and Isaiah 6:8, showing a plurality of persons.Kings I 22:19-22
