  1. C-016Genesis 3:15, the seed of the woman, is the first prophecy of the Messiah.Cites Genesis 3:15 · Galatians 4:4
  2. C-017The promise to Abraham's 'seed' is in the singular and so means one person, Christ.Cites Galatians 3:16 · Genesis 22:18
  3. C-028The Law of Moses was ended or set aside by Christ.Cites Romans 10:4 · Galatians 3:24-25 · Ephesians 2:15 · Hebrews 8:13
  4. C-029Anyone who does not keep every part of the Law is under a curse, and no one can keep it.Cites Galatians 3:10 · Deuteronomy 27:26
  5. C-030Habakkuk 2:4 teaches that the just shall live by faith, not by works of the Law.Cites Romans 1:17 · Galatians 3:11 · Habakkuk 2:4
  6. C-094The promise that all nations would be blessed in Abraham's seed is the gospel preached beforehand.Cites Galatians 3:8 · Acts 3:25 · Genesis 12:3 · Genesis 22:18
  7. C-108Christ became a curse for us, as it is written, 'he that is hanged is accursed of God'.Cites Galatians 3:13 · Deuteronomy 21:22-23
  8. C-119Leviticus 18:5, 'which if a man do, he shall live in them', shows that the Law is not of faith.Cites Galatians 3:12 · Romans 10:5 · Leviticus 18:5
  9. C-121Hagar and Sarah are an allegory of two covenants: Sinai in bondage, and the free children of the promise.Cites Galatians 4:22-31 · Genesis 21:9-12
  10. C-181Circumcision of the flesh is no longer required; true circumcision is of the heart.Cites Galatians 5:2-6 · Romans 2:28-29
  11. C-182The feasts of Israel were shadows fulfilled in Christ and need no longer be kept.Cites Colossians 2:16-17 · Galatians 4:9-10
  12. C-184The Church is the true Israel, and those who believe in Jesus are the children of Abraham.Cites Galatians 6:16 · Romans 9:6-8 · Galatians 3:29 · 1 Peter 2:9-10
