About this site

Each claim has an ID, the verses it cites, and the TaNaKh passages listed against it. It holds verse citations, not commentary.

  • The verses a claim cites are given in the numbering of the King James Version, and quoted from it.
  • TaNaKh passages use the book names and verse numbering of the Chabad.org edition, which sometimes differ from the King James Version; where they do, the passage says so. Each passage links to its chapter on Chabad.org, with Rashi, and to Sefaria.
  • 0 of 586 passages name a source, and 0 of 586 have been verified against that source. A verified passage is marked ✓.

Texts quoted

  • English TaNaKh: The Holy Scriptures According to the Masoretic Text: A New Translation (Jewish Publication Society, 1917). Public domain. Text from Sefaria.
  • Hebrew TaNaKh: Tanach with Nikkud. Public domain. Text from Sefaria.
  • Verses cited by the claims: King James Version. Public domain. Text from bible-api.com.

The JPS 1917 translation is used because it is in the public domain and follows the Hebrew verse numbering. For a modern translation and Rashi's commentary, follow the Chabad.org link beside each passage.

Privacy and safety

This site is plain HTML and one stylesheet. It runs no scripts, sets no cookies, has no forms, collects nothing, and loads nothing from any other website. Each page carries a Content Security Policy that tells the browser to enforce this.
