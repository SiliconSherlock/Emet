C-160

Since God says 'beside me there is no saviour', Jesus the Saviour must be God.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Titus 2:13 KJV

13Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ;

Luke 2:11 KJV

11For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.

Isaiah 43:11 KJV

11I, even I, am the LORD; and beside me there is no saviour.
