C-182

The feasts of Israel were shadows fulfilled in Christ and need no longer be kept.

  • Colossians 2:16-17
  • Galatians 4:9-10

More claims from: Leviticus · Ezekiel · Zechariah · Galatians · Colossians

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Colossians 2:16-17

KJV
16Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days: 17Which are a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ.

Galatians 4:9-10

KJV
9But now, after that ye have known God, or rather are known of God, how turn ye again to the weak and beggarly elements, whereunto ye desire again to be in bondage? 10Ye observe days, and months, and times, and years.
