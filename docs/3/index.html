C-003

Hosea 11:1, 'out of Egypt have I called my son', is a prophecy fulfilled when Jesus returned from Egypt.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Matthew 2:15 KJV

15And was there until the death of Herod: that it might be fulfilled which was spoken of the Lord by the prophet, saying, Out of Egypt have I called my son.

Hosea 11:1 KJV

1When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt.
