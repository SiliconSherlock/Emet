C-023

One righteous person can die to atone for the sins of others.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

1 Peter 3:18 KJV

18For Christ also hath once suffered for sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit:

2 Corinthians 5:21 KJV

21For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.
