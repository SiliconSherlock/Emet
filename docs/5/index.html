C-005

The prophets foretold that the Messiah would be called a Nazarene.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Matthew 2:23 KJV

23And he came and dwelt in a city called Nazareth: that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophets, He shall be called a Nazarene.

The TaNaKh passages

Highlighted verses are the ones the claim cites.

Judges 13:5

The verse the claim cites

No verse in the TaNaKh has the words Matthew quotes. This is one of the verses usually proposed as its source.

5כִּי הִנָּךְ הָרָה וְיֹלַדְתְּ בֵּן וּמוֹרָה לֹא־יַעֲלֶה עַל־רֹאשׁוֹ כִּי־נְזִיר אֱלֹהִים יִהְיֶה הַנַּעַר מִן־הַבָּטֶן וְהוּא יָחֵל לְהוֹשִׁיעַ אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵל מִיַּד פְּלִשְׁתִּים׃
5For, lo, thou shalt conceive, and bear a son; and no razor shall come upon his head; for the child shall be a Nazirite unto God from the womb; and he shall begin to save Israel out of the hand of the Philistines.’
