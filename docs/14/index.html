C-014

Psalm 2:7, 'Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee', speaks of Jesus as the Son of God.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

Acts 13:33 KJV

33God hath fulfilled the same unto us their children, in that he hath raised up Jesus again; as it is also written in the second psalm, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee.

Hebrews 1:5 KJV

5For unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee? And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son?

Psalms 2:7 KJV

7I will declare the decree: the LORD hath said unto me, Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee.
