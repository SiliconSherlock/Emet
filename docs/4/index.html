C-004

Jeremiah 31:15, Rachel weeping for her children, was fulfilled in Herod's killing of the children of Bethlehem.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

Jeremiah 31:15 KJV

15Thus saith the LORD; A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation, and bitter weeping; Rahel weeping for her children refused to be comforted for her children, because they were not.
