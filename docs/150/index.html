C-150

Isaiah 63:9-10, the angel of his presence and his holy Spirit, shows three persons in God.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

Isaiah 63:9-10 KJV

9In all their affliction he was afflicted, and the angel of his presence saved them: in his love and in his pity he redeemed them; and he bare them, and carried them all the days of old. 10But they rebelled, and vexed his holy Spirit: therefore he was turned to be their enemy, and he fought against them.
