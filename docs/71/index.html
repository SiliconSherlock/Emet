C-071

Isaiah 54:13, 'all thy children shall be taught of the LORD', is fulfilled in those who come to Jesus.

UNVERIFIED DRAFT (2026-11-13). Listed from general knowledge of the passages usually cited on each side. Not yet checked against Rabbi Singer or any other rabbinic source, and not attributed to anyone.

What the claim cites

John 6:45 KJV

45It is written in the prophets, And they shall be all taught of God. Every man therefore that hath heard, and hath learned of the Father, cometh unto me.

Isaiah 54:13 KJV

13And all thy children shall be taught of the LORD; and great shall be the peace of thy children.
